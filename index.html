Michele Caselli

I am a postdoctoral researcher in mathematics, currently affiliated with the University of Sydney and Princeton University. My work lies at the intersection of geometric analysis, calculus of variations, and nonlinear partial differential equations.

Email Sydney profile Google Scholar ORCID arXiv CVGMT BibTeX

Research

I study variational and geometric problems involving nonlocal energies. Current themes include fractional perimeter and nonlocal minimal surfaces, fractional Sobolev spaces on manifolds, phase-transition models, sphere-valued Sobolev maps, and regularity questions for elliptic obstacle problems.

  • Geometric analysis
  • Nonlocal PDEs
  • Geometric measure theory
  • Phase transitions
  • Regularity theory
